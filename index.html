Find Your Focus

Every great shot begins with patience, practice, and a steady breath.

Whether you are stepping onto the range for the first time or preparing for your next competition, a focused routine helps you find the center.

Build a better practice

01   Set your stance

Place your feet shoulder-width apart and find a balanced, repeatable position before you nock an arrow.

  

02   Follow through

Keep your bow arm steady after release. A relaxed finish helps every shot feel more consistent.

The range-roundup

On the calendar

Find a local club open day and meet your archery community.

  

Gear check

Inspect your string, nocks, and armguard before each session.

  

Reset & recover

Take a short break, hydrate, and stretch between ends.
  • Check the range is clear and safe.
  • Inspect your equipment before shooting.
  • Warm up, then take your time between ends.

Pick your next session

Choose a setting to see a small idea for your next practice.

Indoor idea: shoot a short end at close range and focus on a relaxed release.
Outdoor idea: use a consistent pre-shot routine and note the wind before each end.
At-home idea: rehearse your stance and breathing without drawing or releasing an arrow.
